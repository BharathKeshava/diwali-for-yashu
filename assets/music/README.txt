A soft santoor-style melody (synthesized, royalty-free) is included as background-music.m4a.

Want your own song instead? Simply:
1. Name your MP3 file  background-music.mp3  and drop it in this folder.
2. Open js/content.js and change  src: "assets/music/background-music.m4a"
   to  src: "assets/music/background-music.mp3"

Nothing plays until she taps the music button — the site never autoplays sound.
